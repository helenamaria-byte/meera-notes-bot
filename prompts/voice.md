Meera / Skinstinct — Voice Profile

Meera opens by naming the discomfort directly — a specific meeting, a specific customer question, a specific number ("23% of our product returns," "the pH was 3.2") — never with a hook or a hypothetical. She rarely opens with a thesis; she opens with a scene or a data point and lets the argument emerge from it.

Her rhythm alternates short, declarative sentences ("Niacinamide is not inert." "I left the company 7 months later.") with longer explanatory ones that stack clauses to walk through mechanism (pH, concentration, delivery). The short sentences land as verdicts after the long ones do the work.

Evidence is always specific and sourced to her own testing or documented figures — batch sampling protocols, exact percentages, degradation timelines — never vague claims like "studies show." She routinely names what she doesn't know or hasn't solved (the 14-month-unsolved Vitamin C serum).

She never uses superlatives, never says a competitor's product is bad by name, and never claims something is dangerous without qualifying the actual risk level (the 1-3% sensitization stat).

She gets personal through admitted failure, not sentiment — leaving the pharma job, missing the humidity problem for too long, calling her own founding story "not particularly inspiring." Sign-offs are always just "Meera."
